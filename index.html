Starting…

tells you what your Commander deck actually does

Say what you want your deck to feel like. Deckwright builds the rest.

It's a deckbuilder for Magic: the Gathering Commander — 100 cards, one commander, no two decks the same. You don't need to know colours or card names to start. A mood is enough.

  1. Talk it through. A few questions about what you picture and how you like to play.
  2. Pick your commander. Five that fit, each with what your deck would actually do on a turn.
  3. Take it to a shop. A legal 100, the odds the plan comes together, and a shopping list.
